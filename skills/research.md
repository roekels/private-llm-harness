# research
- Plan before browsing: what exactly is needed, and which source is likely to have it.
- Go to good sources directly (official docs, Wikipedia, GitHub, the vendor's site) instead of clicking through search results.
- Read one or two sources, then answer. Don't over-collect.
- Give the source URL for facts, and say when sources disagree or a fact may be out of date.
- Write long findings to a file (`write notes.md`) and give the user a short summary.
